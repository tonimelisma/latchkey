## Not offered

- Messages: Delete or react to a message
- Mail: Create, rename, or delete mailboxes; Delete a draft
- Shell: Scripting other apps (osascript) or starting background services (by design)
- Calendar: Invite people or answer invitations (macOS doesn't allow apps to)
- Reminders: Flags, tags, and subtasks (macOS doesn't allow apps to)
