Add to Claude

  1. Click Add to Claude. Claude opens in a new tab (sign in to Claude if it asks) with a form titled “Add custom connector”, already filled in. Claude cautions that the details came from a link; that’s expected, since the link came from Latchkey.
  2. Check that the address is {{breakable .Connector}}, then click Add. Claude shows the settings it found: change nothing, click Add once more, then Connect.
  3. A Latchkey page opens: sign in with your Latchkey passkey (the one you made when you created your account), then click Allow. That lets Claude use your machines.

You’re done once you click Allow. Reload this page: the Claude section then lists Claude. Claude on the web, on your desktop, and on your phone can now reach your machines. Try asking it: “What’s my Mac’s battery level?”

If Add to Claude doesn’t work, add it by hand

On claude.ai, click Customize in the column on the left, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. For the name, type Latchkey. For the address, paste this one. Then click Add and go on as in the steps above.
